# Orbit Overview

Orbit is a multi-tenant scheduling service for field-service teams. It lets dispatchers assign jobs to technicians in real time and gives each technician a mobile view of their route for the day.

The product's headline goals are: cut manual dispatch time by 50%, surface SLA breaches before they happen, and give managers a single dashboard across regions.
